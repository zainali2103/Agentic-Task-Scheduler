⚠ Interpretation Note

The absolute TCR values (~38–42%) reflect a deliberate dataset design choice: 80.5% of scenarios are infeasible, meaning total task duration exceeds the 600-minute workday. No scheduler can complete all tasks under this constraint. The meaningful measure is relative performance. The DQN's +11.8pp advantage on high-complexity scenarios demonstrates genuine learned adaptive prioritisation — the most valuable capability in real-world scheduling.
